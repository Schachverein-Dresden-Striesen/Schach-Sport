# Glossary: Schach-Sport

## Physiology

The study of how the human body works in service of chess performance. In this repo: sleep, nutrition, hydration, movement, mental resilience, stress management, and immune health. **Excludes**: injury treatment or disability accommodation (medical territory; see disclaimer in README).

## Chess Performance (Holistic)

The intersection of playing strength, tournament endurance, decision quality, and stress recovery. These are interconnected—improving one typically improves others. Not isolated dimensions.

**Related**: Playing Strength, Tournament Endurance, Decision Quality, Stress Recovery.

## Playing Strength

Chess rating, calculation depth, opening knowledge, position evaluation. The traditional measure of "how good" a player is. In this repo: influenced by mental fatigue, cardiovascular fitness, and sleep.

## Tournament Endurance

The ability to play well across multiple games (5+ rounds in a single day or multi-day event) without mental fatigue degrading decision quality. Core measure of physiology impact on chess.

## Decision Quality

The clarity and soundness of moves chosen under time pressure. Degrades with stress, fatigue, dehydration, and poor sleep.

## Stress Recovery

The physiological and mental return to baseline after a tournament game, training session, or stressful period. Shorter recovery = more resilience for the next round.

## Immune Resilience

The body's ability to resist illness and recover quickly. During tournament seasons with travel and close quarters, immune health directly impacts attendance and performance consistency.

## Mental Resilience

Emotional stability, focus, motivation, and the ability to handle pressure without panic or negative self-talk. Shaped by sleep, exercise, breathing techniques, and stress management practices.

## Content Topic

A single article covering one physiology aspect (e.g., "Sleep for Chess Performance," "Nutrition for Tournaments"). Exists in two languages: English (`topic.en.md`) and German (`topic.de.md`).

## Bilingual Parity

The state where a Content Topic's English and German versions cover the same science, examples, and practical tips—even if wording differs for cultural or linguistic reasons. A goal to track, not always achievable simultaneously.

## Ready to Publish

A Content Topic has been reviewed for accuracy (science), clarity (reader understanding), and—if translated—bilingual parity. Approved by at least one reviewer before merge to main.
